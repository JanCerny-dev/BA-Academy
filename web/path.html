Learning path

Plan for about 8–10 hours a week. It mixes theory and practice every week: read, watch, then do something in a real tool. The capstone project grows alongside the modules, so by the end you have a portfolio, not just notes.

Essentials route

Do the steps marked Essentials, read the Quick version of every other module, and in the capstone do steps 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9 and 10. Deep dives only for BPMN (04), specifications (06), user stories (07), SQL (10) and APIs (11) – those are explicit in the job ad. Roughly 100–120 hours.

Why this order

  • Role and delivery first – everything else makes sense once you know who does what in an IT project.
  • Process modeling early – it builds on what you already do and gives quick wins in a real tool.
  • Requirements before technology – SQL and APIs are tools for checking and specifying requirements, not goals in themselves.
  • Testing, low-code and AI late – they need the earlier pieces (requirements, data, APIs) to make sense.
  • Interview prep at the end, applications earlier – once the Essentials steps are done you can start applying; keep going with the rest while you interview.
