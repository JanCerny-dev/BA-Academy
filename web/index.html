Self-study path

From process improver to IT Business Analyst

You already spot processes that could run better, push automation through IT, get it approved and test it. That is a large part of business analysis, done by instinct. This path adds the names, methods and tools behind it, so you can walk into an interview, talk about the work with confidence and show things you have actually built.

overall progress
Essentials route~10–12 weeks · quick versions of everything, deep dives where the ad is explicit, core courses, a lighter capstone. Full route~24 weeks at 8–10 h/week · every module in depth, all core courses, the full capstone portfolio.

How it works

  1. Read each module in the Quick version (what it is, how to explain it) or the Deep dive (the full theory). Priorities P1–P3 tell you what matters most.
  2. Watch the matching Udemy course or free videos. Tick them off as you go.
  3. Do the hands-on exercises in free tools – BPMN, SQL, APIs, Jira, automation.
  4. Build the capstone: one fictional company, ExportShield Insurance, carried from problem to automation. It becomes your interview portfolio.
  5. Practise the interview questions until you can answer them out loud.
