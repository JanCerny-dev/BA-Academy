Positions this path prepares you for

Real job ads (autumn 2026) showing the kind of roles the path targets. Ads expire quickly – use them to see what employers ask for, then search for current ones on jobs.cz, startupjobs.cz, LinkedIn and company career pages with keywords like business analyst, IT analytik, procesní analytik, Power Platform, junior.

Typical role titles

  • IT Business Analyst / IT analytik – requirements to specifications, works with vendors and testers. The main target.
  • Business Analyst (junior) – same core, sometimes closer to business than IT.
  • Process analyst / procesní analytik – BPMN, process improvement, automation candidates.
  • Low-code / Power Platform analyst – automation with Power Automate/Power Apps.
  • Product Owner (junior) – backlog and stories; usually after 1–2 years as a BA.
  • IT project coordinator / junior PM – delivery, vendors, status reporting; close cousin of the BA role.
