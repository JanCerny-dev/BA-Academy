Udemy courses

Selected (October 2026) by fit with the job ad, rating (mostly ≥ 4.5), freshness and length. Core courses (13, about 62 hours of video) carry the path; optional ones go deeper or offer an alternative. You do not need to finish every course: skip sections you already know, and do the exercises on this site instead of passive watching.

Paid courses

Courses on udemy.com are paid individually. Udemy runs discounts very often, so a course rarely costs the list price – wait for a sale. If your employer has Udemy Business, the same courses are usually included there for free; ask HR or your learning team. Ratings and hours come from the course pages in October 2026.
