Certificates

Honest summary: for a junior IT Business Analyst in the Czech Republic no certificate is a hard requirement. Employers look for evidence that you can analyse, model and specify – which is what the capstone portfolio shows. A certificate helps as a tie-breaker, as structure for your learning, and in some banks and consultancies that value a common vocabulary. Prices and formats were checked in October 2026; verify before you pay.

IIBA ECBA – Entry Certificate in Business Analysis P2 · Should know

The most natural first BA certificate. Cheap during the current promotion. It shows knowledge, not experience – pair it with the portfolio.

Issued byIIBA (International Institute of Business Analysis)
LevelEntry level, no prerequisites (no experience, no training hours, no application)
CostUSD 316 for non-members / USD 196 for members, including the exam and one year of membership. A 20 % discount runs 29 Oct – 31 Dec 2026.
FormatOnline proctored exam on the BABOK Guide, available in six languages.
Weight in CZ hiringRarely required in Czech ads, but recognised internationally and in banks and consultancies with global ties. It proves you know the standard vocabulary.
When it makes senseAfter the Core BA craft phase, if you want a formal proof of BA knowledge. Udemy has a prep course (Pass the ECBA).

IREB CPRE Foundation Level – Certified Professional for Requirements Engineering P2 · Should know

Equal alternative to ECBA, more focused on writing requirements. The syllabus is free and worth reading even without the exam.

Issued byIREB (International Requirements Engineering Board); exams run by certification bodies such as GASQ
LevelFoundation, no prerequisites
CostSet by the certification body; e.g. GASQ lists a remote exam at around EUR 250 + VAT. No Czech price list found – ask the provider.
FormatMultiple-choice exam on the free CPRE syllabus (v3.3.0); online exams available through certifiers.
Weight in CZ hiringStrong in German-speaking and Central European companies, banks and public sector IT. Fits the 'specifications for vendors' part of the job closely.
When it makes senseIf the target employers are banks, insurers or companies working with German/Austrian groups.

Scrum.org PSPO I – Professional Scrum Product Owner P3 · Nice to have

Good value, but optional. For a BA it is more relevant than the Scrum Master version (PSM I).

Issued byScrum.org
LevelFoundation, no prerequisites, no course required
CostUSD 200 per attempt, one-time payment; the test password does not expire.
Format80 questions, 60 minutes, 85 % to pass, online.
Weight in CZ hiringKnown in agile teams; helps if the employer runs Scrum and the BA works closely with the Product Owner.
When it makes senseAfter module 07, if job ads you target mention Scrum or the Product Owner role.

Official page

ISTQB CTFL – Certified Tester Foundation Level (v4.0) P3 · Nice to have

Useful bonus, not a BA certificate. Worth it if you also consider a test analyst role as an entry route.

Issued byISTQB, in the Czech Republic through CaSTB and training providers
LevelFoundation, no prerequisites
CostAround CZK 3,800 excl. VAT for the exam alone at Gopas (price list from August 2025); courses are charged separately. No official 2026 CaSTB price found.
Format40 questions, 60 minutes.
Weight in CZ hiringVery well known in Czech IT. The ad asks you to support testers and test deliveries – CTFL proves you speak their language.
When it makes senseIf the role leans towards testing and acceptance, or as a way into IT through a test analyst role.

Microsoft PL-900 – Power Platform Fundamentals P3 · Nice to have

Nice proof of low-code literacy; a working automation in your portfolio is worth more.

Issued byMicrosoft
LevelFundamentals
CostMicrosoft fundamentals exam; the price depends on the country – check on the official page before booking (not verified for CZ).
FormatOnline or test centre exam on Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI and Dataverse concepts.
Weight in CZ hiringRelevant only where the employer uses Power Platform for low-code automation – as in the target ad.
When it makes senseAfter module 14, if you aim at automation-heavy roles. Udemy has a prep course.

OMG OCEB 2 Fundamental – business process management P3 · Nice to have

Skip for now. BPMN shown in a portfolio is worth more to Czech employers.

Issued byOMG (Object Management Group)
LevelFundamental
CostUSD 350 + taxes via Pearson VUE.
FormatPearson VUE exam on BPM and BPMN.
Weight in CZ hiringLittle known in Czech job ads.
When it makes senseOnly for a strong process/BPM specialisation later.

IIBA CCBA / CBAP – experienced BA certificates P3 · Nice to have

Not now. Mentioned so you know the IIBA ladder: ECBA → CCBA → CBAP.

Issued byIIBA
LevelRequires 2 years (CCBA) or 5 years (CBAP) of BA work experience
CostSee IIBA.
FormatApplication with documented experience, then exam.
Weight in CZ hiringRespected, but you cannot take it as a junior.
When it makes senseYears 2–5 of your BA career.
